# Rune Factory 5 Rigbarth Maze Early Challenge

## Overview

This case study documents an early clear of Rigbarth Maze Floor 20 shortly after completing the main story.

Rather than rushing the dungeon immediately, I spent a short preparation period improving my equipment and crafting capabilities to maximize efficiency.

Unlike a conventional post-game challenge, I also continued my daily friendship farming throughout the entire process instead of focusing solely on dungeon progression.

---

## Play Conditions

* New Game+
* Hard Difficulty
* DLC Starter Set Used

---

## Objectives

* Clear Rigbarth Maze Floor 20 as early as possible.
* Minimize preparation time after the main story.
* Maintain daily friendship farming during the challenge.

---

## Preparation

After clearing the main story in Spring, I immediately began preparing for Rigbarth Maze.

I spent one night raising my Crafting level, obtaining Recipe Bread, and collecting Melody Bottles and 10-Fold Steel for weapon upgrades.

Around 6 PM the following day, I entered Rigbarth Maze carrying a Winter Rod.

---

## Progression

After obtaining a Dragonic Stone on Floor 14, I immediately returned to Rigbarth to craft a Magical Rod.

The weapon was upgraded using Melody Bottles, 10-Fold Steel, and a Rare Can before re-entering the dungeon.

From there, I climbed to Floor 20 using Peace Rosary to avoid unnecessary encounters while relying on Gatling Comet for mandatory battles.

---

## Key Strategies

* One-night preparation before entering the maze
* Winter Rod → Magical Rod upgrade
* Melody Bottles + 10-Fold Steel + Rare Can upgrades
* Peace Rosary for encounter avoidance
* Gatling Comet for mandatory combat

---

## Results

Rigbarth Maze Floor 20 was cleared almost immediately after it became available.

The clear was achieved around **1:34 AM on Spring 29**.

A separate verification screenshot taken after re-entering the dungeon for documentation was recorded at approximately **2:30 AM**, confirming the completion.

Although this challenge was never part of my original Spring story clear route, it became one of the most memorable parts of the entire playthrough.

Daily friendship farming also continued throughout the challenge.

Instead of optimizing exclusively for dungeon progression, I maintained my daily friendship routine with nearly every permanent resident while preparing for and clearing Rigbarth Maze.

This playthrough demonstrates that long-term relationship building and aggressive early-game progression are not necessarily conflicting objectives.

---

## Lessons Learned

The challenge demonstrated that a short but focused preparation period could significantly reduce the overall difficulty of an early Rigbarth Maze clear.

Rather than rushing immediately after unlocking the dungeon, investing a single night into crafting and equipment upgrades resulted in a much smoother progression while still preserving long-term gameplay efficiency.
