# Rune Factory 5 Spring 4–5 Copper Route

## Overview

This document records an early-game mining route that allows practical Copper acquisition as early as **Spring 5**.

Although the area itself is accessible on **Spring 4**, Spring 5 is generally the earliest practical timing because a free Hammer becomes available on that day.

The route is currently maintained as a **Discovery Asset** for future publication.

---

## Route

**Location**

* Northeastern corner of **Phoros Woodlands**
* Near the Wanted Monster **Cluckadoodle**

Although higher-level enemies patrol the area, the route is relatively safe once you know the path.

A save point is also located nearby at **Bandit King's Old Base**.

---

## Requirements

* Reach Phoros Woodlands
* Hammer (Spring 5 free Hammer recommended)

---

## Materials Obtainable

* Copper Ore
* Iron Ore
* Amethyst Ore
* Stone
* Rune Crystal (RP Recovery)
* Nearby Save Point (Bandit King's Old Base)

---

## Practical Uses

* Early Copper weapon crafting
* Early equipment upgrades
* Stable RP recovery via Rune Crystal
* Convenient save point for repeated mining runs
