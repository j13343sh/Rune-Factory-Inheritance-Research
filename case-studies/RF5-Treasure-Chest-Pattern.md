# Rune Factory 5 Treasure Chest Pattern

## Overview

This document summarizes the treasure chest item distribution observed during a New Game+ playthrough.

While exploring the early game, a repeating distribution pattern became apparent among treasure chests containing Orange Juice, Healing Potion, Tomato Seeds, and Recipe Bread.

Although the exact internal selection algorithm remains unknown, the observed pattern was highly consistent throughout the route.

---

## Observation

A repeating three-chest cycle was observed.

* Two consecutive chests contained **4 Orange Juice**.
* The following chest contained **1 Healing Potion**, **9 Tomato Seeds**, and **9 Recipe Bread**.

This cycle repeated consistently throughout the observed treasure chests.
